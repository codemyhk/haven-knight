HAVEN KNIGHT is a rpg roguelike game made for thr haven hackathon about the topic: bounce back.

the game has a melee and a gunner who both have customs weapons and sets for their passives.

evertyime you beat a dungeon which includes 7 floors and a boss, you get a permanent power up

the game has 3 dungeons with three bosses that get progressively harder. 

use your mouse to aim your weapon

theres a sniper, smg, pistol, etc with different builds that can be made for different purposes
